# Product brief

## Objective

Help users compare audio equipment by **reproducible evidence** and usage-specific requirements, without subjective or marketing-based false precision.

## Audiences

Audio enthusiasts and engineering-oriented consumers. Interface is Japanese-first.

## Core capabilities

1. Product catalog with filters and provenance.
2. Side-by-side comparison of compatible measurements.
3. Optional user-weighted decision aid and visible data coverage.
4. Amp–speaker pairing: SPL/power headroom, load/stability status, frequency-dependent electrical effects, limitations.
5. Data methodology, editorial principles, exported results.

## UX baseline

White layout, compact scientific-data presentation, fixed left pane / mobile navigation. No big gradient hero, decorative dashboard clutter, or arbitrary unified quality score. For pairing: a large primary output assessment followed by three cards and collapsible calculation details, colored blue/red/black by result semantics.

## Non-goals in MVP

Automated scraping, definitive subjective tonal synergy, real-time price completeness, safety certification, medical-like confidence claims, hosting on AWS, or ranking every audio device.
